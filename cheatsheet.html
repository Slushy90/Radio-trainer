HAM LINGO
What people actually say on the air.
EVERYDAY LINGO
CQ
Calling anyone. “Is anybody out there?”
CQ DX
Calling long-distance stations only.
DE
“From” or “this is”, before a callsign.
K / KN
Over, go ahead. KN means named station only.
AR
End of message.
SK
End of contact. Signing off.
73
Best wishes. The usual goodbye.
88
Love and kisses. Friends and family.
DX
Long distance. Far-away stations.
RST
Signal report: Readability, Strength, Tone.
59 / 599
Perfect report. 59 on voice, 599 on Morse.
GM GA GE
Good morning, afternoon, evening.
TNX / TU
Thanks. Thank you.
PSE / AGN
Please. Again, say it again.
FB
Fine business. Great, well done.
OM / YL
Man / woman operator.
WX
Weather.
SWL
Shortwave listener. Someone who only listens.
Shack
Your radio room. Rig is your radio.
Elmer
A mentor who helps newcomers.
Pile-up
Many stations calling one rare station at once.

A FIRST CONTACT, LINE BY LINE
CQ CQ CQ DE [YOUR CALL] [YOUR CALL] K
Calling anyone, this is me. Over.
[THEIR CALL] DE [YOUR CALL] GM UR RST 59 QTH [TOWN] HW? KN
Answering. Good morning, you are 59, I am in [town]. How copy?
[YOUR CALL] DE [THEIR CALL] R TNX UR RST 59 73 AR
Received, thanks, you are 59 too. Best wishes, end of message.
[THEIR CALL] DE [YOUR CALL] 73 SK
Best wishes. End of contact.
